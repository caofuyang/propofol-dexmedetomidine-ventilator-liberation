# Target-Trial Specification and Operational Emulation

| Protocol component | Hypothetical pragmatic trial | Operational emulation in MIMIC-IV 3.1 | Remaining deviation or interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical question | Among invasively ventilated adults receiving propofol at a liberation decision point, what is the effect of completing transition to dexmedetomidine versus continuing propofol? | Repeated comparison at 6-hour landmarks after the start of a propofol episode | Clinical intent and the exact bedside decision are incompletely recorded |
| Eligibility | Age ≥18 years; invasive ventilation; receiving propofol; no prior dexmedetomidine in the episode; no prior extubation, tracheostomy, death, or ICU discharge | Applied at 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, and 48 hours using medication, ventilation, procedure, location, and death records; invasive-ventilation coverage is also required at window end | Eligibility relies on electronic proxies and conditions the contrast on remaining ventilated and observable through the window |
| Time zero | The treatment decision point | Landmark time defines eligibility; completed strategy is classified during the next 6 hours | Primary follow-up does not begin at time zero; it is conditional on surviving and remaining intubated through classification |
| Transition strategy | Initiate dexmedetomidine and discontinue propofol within 6 hours | Dexmedetomidine starts in `[landmark, landmark+6 h)`, no more than 2 hours after the propofol episode ends; propofol ends by window end and no later than 6 hours after dexmedetomidine starts; a raw dexmedetomidine record covers window end | Dose targets, titration, and clinical reason are not standardized |
| Continuation strategy | Continue propofol without dexmedetomidine for 6 hours | No dexmedetomidine in the window and the union of raw propofol records covers the entire interval from landmark through window end without an initial, internal, or terminal gap | Electronic coverage cannot prove bedside delivery; treatment changes after the window are permitted in the primary point-strategy estimand |
| Assignment | Random allocation at time zero | Observed assignment; adjusted with propensity-score overlap weighting | Exchangeability is not guaranteed; readiness and intent confounding remain likely |
| Follow-up | Ideally begins at treatment assignment and continues through outcome horizon | Begins at the end of the 6-hour classification window among instances still covered by invasive-ventilation records; primary event assessed over the next 48 hours, failure over 72 hours after extubation | Estimates a completed-strategy landmark contrast, not an intention-to-treat effect from window start; post-landmark conditioning can select trajectories differently by strategy |
| Primary outcome | Successful planned extubation without recurrent invasive ventilation, tracheostomy, or death | Documented extubation within 48 hours, no unplanned-extubation code anywhere in that outcome window, and no hospital-admission-level reintubation code, invasive-ventilation restart, tracheostomy, or death within 72 hours | The extubation code does not independently verify clinical planning; structured failure codes may remain incomplete; live discharge ends in-hospital ascertainment |
| Safety outcomes | Unplanned extubation, bradycardia, hypotension, and vasopressor escalation | Code-defined unplanned extubation; ≥2 post-window HR or MAP readings at prespecified thresholds; new/additional vasopressor class | Safety definitions were refined post hoc and depend on measurement frequency |
| Causal contrast | Per-protocol or intention-to-treat effect in the randomized eligible population | Adjusted marginal risk difference and risk ratio in the propensity-overlap population of observed completed strategies | Must be described as an adjusted association because key causal assumptions are not verifiable |
| Analysis | Randomized comparison with subject-level inference | Logistic propensity score; overlap weights; 300 nonparametric subject-cluster bootstrap samples; primary bootstrap refits variable screening, imputation, scaling, encoding, and the propensity model | Repeated trial instances target eligible decisions, not a single patient-level intervention |
| Readiness analysis | Restrict or stratify using prospectively standardized liberation criteria | Low-settings, enhanced-readiness, documented-successful-SBT, and six-variable complete-readiness analyses | Readiness measurements are incomplete and subgroup definitions were refined during audit |
| Missing data | Prospectively complete core readiness variables or use prespecified handling | Variables observed in ≥20% retained; numeric median imputation plus missing indicators; categorical imputation | Single imputation does not propagate imputation uncertainty or restore unrecorded assessments |
| Sensitivity analyses | Prespecified alternative timing and model assumptions | First decision per stay, early landmarks, complete respiratory/readiness data, narrower timing, individual landmarks, spline-logit propensity model, and a no-later-dexmedetomidine diagnostic among continuation instances | Multiple supportive analyses are exploratory; the last analysis conditions on future treatment and is not an alternative causal estimand |
| External transportability | Replicate the same strategy and outcome in independent centers | eICU uses intermittent infusion records and an oxygen-device proxy; common support contains 45 transitions | Non-equivalent and sparse; classified only as exploratory directional consistency |

## Recommended manuscript placement

Submit this as a main-methods table if the journal permits, otherwise as Supplementary Table S1. It makes the principal estimand limitation visible: the implemented analysis compares completed strategies after a 6-hour assessment window and is not equivalent to randomization at the start of that window.
